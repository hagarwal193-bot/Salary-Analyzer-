FAQ: CTC, PF, LWF and In-Hand Salary

Answers to common questions about CTC, in-hand salary, PF, Labour Welfare Fund and how Salary Analyzer works.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CTC the same as my monthly salary?

No. CTC is an annual figure representing the company's total cost, including parts you don't receive as cash monthly (like employer PF and gratuity). Your monthly in-hand salary is always lower than CTC ÷ 12.

Does every state charge Labour Welfare Fund?

No, LWF applicability, contribution amount, and collection frequency differ by state. Some states like Uttar Pradesh and Delhi have no LWF scheme at all, while others collect it monthly or half-yearly.

Why is my employer's PF contribution part of my CTC?

Employers structure CTC to reflect their complete cost of employing you, which includes their own statutory contributions on your behalf, even though that money doesn't appear in your bank account each month.

Can I reduce my tax deduction legally?

Yes, through eligible exemptions and deductions under the old tax regime (like HRA exemption, Section 80C investments, and NPS contributions) or by comparing your tax liability under the new regime. This tool gives an estimate; a tax advisor can help optimise further.

Why did my ESI deduction suddenly stop?

ESI applies only while your gross monthly salary is at or below the ₹21,000 threshold. Once a salary hike takes you above that limit, the deduction (and the employer's matching contribution) stops from the next contribution period onward — it isn't an error on your payslip.

Should I choose the old or new tax regime?

It depends on how many deductions you claim. If you have a home loan, active 80C investments, or significant HRA exemption, the old regime often works out better. With few deductions, the new regime's lower slab rates usually win. Use the calculator to compare your actual numbers under both before deciding.

Do I get gratuity if I leave before 5 years?

For regular (permanent) employees, generally no — gratuity becomes payable after five years of continuous service, except in cases of death or disablement, where the five-year condition is waived. Under the Code on Social Security, 2020 (in force since 21 November 2025), fixed-term employees can qualify for pro-rata gratuity after one year of continuous service. Check your appointment letter and confirm with your HR team.

Why do two offers with the same CTC pay differently in-hand?

Because CTC is a total-cost figure, not a cash figure. Two companies can split the same CTC very differently between fixed pay, employer PF, gratuity accrual, insurance, and variable bonus — and only the fixed cash portion shows up in your monthly in-hand salary.

Yes — the Custom Salary Builder mode lets HR and payroll teams adjust individual components per employee and export a formatted salary slip as PDF or Excel, making it useful for quick what-if structuring during offer rollouts.
