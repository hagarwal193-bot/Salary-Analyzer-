Privacy Policy

What this site collects, what it does not, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Salary Analyzer (“we”, “this site”) collects when you visit salaryanalyzer.in, how it is used, and the choices you have. The calculator itself does not require you to sign up, log in or submit any personal information. The figures you type into it (CTC, allowances, state and so on) are processed inside your own browser and are not sent to, or stored on, our servers.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, PAN, bank details or employer details in order to use the calculator, and we do not sell personal information.

Local storage

Your browser’s local storage is used only to remember display preferences such as light or dark theme. This stays on your device.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand overall traffic, such as pages viewed, approximate location (city or region), device and browser type. Google Analytics uses cookies or similar identifiers and may collect your IP address. We use this information in aggregate to improve the site; we do not use it to identify you personally.

Advertising and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to show advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses and other identifiers to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. Third parties may therefore place and read cookies on your browser, or use web beacons or IP addresses, to collect information as a result of ad serving on this site. Advertisements are provided by Google and are separate from our own content. You can learn more in How Google uses data when you use our partners’ sites or apps.

Your choices

You can opt out of personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings or, for other ad networks, at www.aboutads.info. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features of websites may then work differently. You can opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it last changed.

Questions about this policy

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our Contact page.
