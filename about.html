About Us

Salary Analyzer was built to remove the guesswork from Indian salary structuring. Designed and developed by Harsh Agarwal, this free tool is aimed at HR professionals, job seekers, and salaried employees who want a clear, instant breakdown of their CTC into real, in-hand take-home pay — including often-overlooked components like state-specific Labour Welfare Fund contributions. The goal is simple: make salary structures understandable to everyone, not just payroll specialists.
